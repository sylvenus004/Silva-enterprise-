Our Background

Silva Entreprizes was established in 2019 as a cyber cafe. Since its establishment, the business has developed steadily into one of the largest electronic and software companies.

Our History

The business was established to cater for the emerging need of technological and internet services by the population in Njoro and its environs and beyond.

Over the years, it has expanded its services as a result of the fast-changing technological world and the need to adapt to the new trends.
